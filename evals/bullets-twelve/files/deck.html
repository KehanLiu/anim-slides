Twelve things to know about octopuses

  • Three hearts: two pump blood through the gills, one through the body
  • Blue blood, because copper-based haemocyanin carries the oxygen
  • About two thirds of the neurons sit in the arms
  • No bones; the only hard part is the beak
  • Fits through any gap larger than its beak
  • Ink clouds hide an escape
  • Suckers can taste what they touch
  • Most species live one to two years
  • Females guard their eggs and stop eating
  • The mimic octopus imitates lionfish and sea snakes
  • Some carry coconut shells as portable shelters
  • The plural is octopuses
