Where you can speak up

  • Petition: 300 signatures put an idea on the agenda
  • Committee hearing: three minutes per speaker
  • Written comments: open until the second reading

Ordinance or resolution?

Ordinance

A local law

Binding on everyone in the city

Two readings and a vote

Enforced with fines

Resolution

A formal opinion or decision

Binding on the council only

One reading and a vote

Not enforceable
