Election day at Polling Station 14

Roles, rules and the count

Before you go

  • Check your shift on the rota
  • Bring a packed lunch; the station runs 15 hours
  • Phones stay in your bag inside the room

Three people, three roles

Voter

  • Brings ID or a poll card
  • Signs the register, takes one ballot
  • Must not photograph a marked ballot

Poll worker

  • Opens the station at 7:00
  • Checks the name, issues the ballot
  • Must not suggest a choice

Observer

  • Shows an accreditation badge
  • Watches issuing and counting
  • Must not touch ballots or talk to voters
Every ballot is handled in view of at least two people.
