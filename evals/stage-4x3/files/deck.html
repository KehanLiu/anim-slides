How tap water is made

Five stages between the reservoir and your kitchen

Five treatment stages

1

Coagulation

Coagulant makes fine particles clump

2

Flocculation

Slow mixing grows the clumps into floc

3

Sedimentation

Heavy floc settles to the tank floor

4

Filtration

Sand and carbon beds catch what is left

5

Disinfection

Chlorine or UV kills remaining microbes

Did you know?

  • The plant treats about 90 million litres on a summer day
  • Water spends roughly six hours in the plant
  • Samples are tested every four hours
