History of medicine · Lecture 4

Two centuries of vaccines

From cowpox to mRNA

Why look back

  • Every vaccine solved a different technical problem
  • Eradication took 184 years from the first trial
  • The pace sped up as the science matured

Milestones

  1. 1796 Jenner tests cowpox inoculation against smallpox
  2. 1885 Pasteur's rabies vaccine saves Joseph Meister
  3. 1896 First killed typhoid vaccines
  4. 1921 BCG against tuberculosis first given to people
  5. 1923 Ramon develops diphtheria toxoid
  6. 1926 Whole-cell pertussis vaccine
  7. 1937 Theiler's 17D yellow fever vaccine
  8. 1945 First influenza vaccine licensed in the US
  9. 1955 Salk's inactivated polio vaccine licensed
  10. 1963 First measles vaccine licensed
  11. 1971 Combined MMR vaccine licensed
  12. 1980 WHO declares smallpox eradicated
  13. 1986 First recombinant vaccine (hepatitis B)
  14. 2006 First HPV vaccine licensed
  15. 2020 First mRNA vaccines authorised
“The annihilation of the smallpox must be the final result of this practice.”
Edward Jenner, 1801
