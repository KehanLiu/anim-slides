The four phases of the cell cycle

  • Step 1, G1: the cell grows and makes proteins
  • Step 2, S: the DNA is copied
  • Step 3, G2: the cell checks the copy for errors
  • Step 4, M: the cell divides into two daughter cells
  • Each daughter cell then re-enters G1, and the cycle starts again
