Lowering the speed limit in a town centre

  1. The council lowers the speed limit in the town centre
  2. Drivers lose a few minutes on each trip
  3. Fewer pedestrians are hurt, and injuries are less severe
  4. The council's conclusion: the safety gain outweighs the lost minutes

Of every 100 litres of water on Earth

  • First, 97 litres are salt water, almost all of it in the oceans
  • Then 2 litres are frozen in ice caps and glaciers
  • Then 1 litre is liquid fresh water, mostly under the ground

Rounded from USGS Water Science School, "Where is Earth's water?": about 97 % saline, about 1.7 % ice caps and glaciers, under 1 % liquid fresh water.

Where your genes are kept

  • A gene is a stretch of DNA
  • Then the DNA is wound up into a chromosome
  • Then the chromosomes lie inside the nucleus
  • Then the nucleus is part of the cell
