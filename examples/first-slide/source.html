How a package gets delivered

  • Order placed: the customer checks out online and receives a confirmation e-mail with a tracking number
  • Picked at the warehouse: staff locate the item (aisle 14), pack it, label it and scan it out
  • Sorted at the regional hub: parcels are scanned and routed onto trucks by postcode
  • Out for delivery: the parcel is loaded onto a local van with about 30 other stops
  • Delivered: the driver hands it over, collects a signature and the status changes to “Delivered”
  • Typical lead time: about 28 hours from order to doorstep
